Here's How Long Your Food Actually Lasts

-July 12th 2025

How Long Can You Keep Leftovers?

Many people keep leftovers in the fridge for convenience, but how long are they actually safe to eat? According to Mayo Clinic, leftovers should only be stored in the refrigerator for 3 to 4 days. Beyond that, the risk of harmful bacteria like Listeria and Salmonella increases—even if the food still smells and looks fine. If you don’t think you’ll eat your leftovers in time, freeze them immediately. While frozen leftovers are safe indefinitely, it’s best to eat them within 3 to 4 months for the best flavor and texture.

Beware of the “Danger Zone” as food becomes unsafe quickly when left at temperatures between 40°F and 140°F (4°C–60°C). Bacteria multiply rapidly here, which can lead to foodborne illness. Don’t leave perishable food out for more than 2 hours If the room is hotter than 32°C (like outdoors), reduce that to 1 hour

When you reheat leftovers, make sure the food reaches 165°F (74°C) throughout. Use a food thermometer if you have one. To help the food heat evenly, stir halfway through if you’re microwaving, and reheat in shallow containers rather than deep ones.

Mayo Clinic recommends a conservative approach to leftovers: refrigerate within 2 hours, consume within 3–4 days, or freeze right away. Always reheat thoroughly and don’t rely on taste or smell to assess safety—these measures help prevent foodborne illness and reduce waste. Mayo Clinic Article

Refrigerator Storage of Fresh or Uncooked Products

Product Storage Times After Purchase
Poultry 1 or 2 days
Beef, Veal, Pork and Lamb 3 to 5 days
Ground Meat and Ground Poultry 1 or 2 days
Fresh Variety Meats (Liver, Tongue, Brain, Kidneys, Heart, Chitterlings) 1 or 2 days
Cured Ham, Cook-Before-Eating 5 to 7 days
Sausage from Pork, Beef or Turkey, Uncooked 1 or 2 days
Eggs 3 to 5 weeks
