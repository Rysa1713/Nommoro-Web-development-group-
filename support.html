Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save a recipe?
Click the "Save" button on any recipe page. It will appear in your dashboard.

How does the expiry calculator work?
Enter the food item and its expiry date. We'll track and notify you when it's close to expiring.

Can I hide my profile?
Yes. Go to Privacy Settings and set your profile visibility to “Private.”
